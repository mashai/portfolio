Hello Games brought me and a colleague in to fix Joe Danger Touch while their team was shipping No Man's Sky. The game was one of the App Store's original smash hits — Editors' Choice, 9/10s from IGN, Eurogamer, and PocketGamer — but had developed game-breaking rendering and localisation issues.

Most of the code had originated in a previous engine with its own rendering system, hooked into Unity with no documentation. I diagnosed the problems by black-boxing the subsystems the game relied on, rebuilt the Unity hooks, and rewrote the shaders. Following the fix I ported the title to Android TV and Android Box, adding controller support and all required platform adaptations.
