Joe Danger Touch is a mobile platform racing game by Hello Games. I was brought in to diagnose and resolve game-breaking issues affecting rendering materials and localisation, working with legacy engine integrations that lacked documentation or accessible source context.

I stabilised the integration by black-boxing subsystems, rebuilding Unity hooks, and rewriting shaders. Following that engagement I ported the title to Android TV and Android Box, adding controller support and all platform-specific adaptations required for release.
