Playlists.net is a Spotify companion app for discovering music on the move, featuring Playlist Charts, Reviews, and New Release listings. At Fluid Pixel, I was responsible for developing the app, integrating Spotify's C++ library in close collaboration with its author, and working alongside an iOS designer.
