Playlists.net (formerly Share My Playlists) is a Spotify companion app designed to make playlist discovery as frictionless as possible, surfacing music through a variety of exploration methods. Developed at Fluid Pixel Ltd, I contributed to features, stability, and delivery of the app across iOS and Android.
