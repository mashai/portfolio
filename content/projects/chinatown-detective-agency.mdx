Chinatown Detective Agency is a cybernoir point-and-click adventure blending retro design with innovative mechanics. I joined General Interactive Co mid-production as Lead Programmer to scale and stabilise development across a multidisciplinary team spanning engineering, design, art, audio, and writing.

I scoped, implemented, and shipped new gameplay systems and missions on the existing codebase, improving robustness and reusability while resolving production-blocking bugs. Alongside the hands-on engineering work, I took on technical leadership responsibilities — onboarding engineers, guiding implementation decisions, and unblocking the team to meet delivery milestones through to release on Steam.
