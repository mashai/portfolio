Chinatown Detective Agency is a cybernoir point-and-click set in 2037 Singapore, where players investigate cases using real-world internet research. I joined General Interactive mid-production as Lead Programmer to scale and stabilise development across a multidisciplinary team spanning engineering, design, art, audio, and writing.

I scoped, implemented, and shipped new gameplay systems and missions on the existing codebase, improving robustness and reusability while resolving production-blocking bugs. I guided implementation decisions, onboarded incoming engineers, and kept the team aligned across disciplines. Unfortunately budget constraints meant I had to move on before the game reached the level of polish it deserved.
