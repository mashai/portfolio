A personal portfolio used as a live experiment in AI-assisted development — less about the output and more about refining the process of working with an AI coding agent effectively.

The focus was on testing AI capabilities and establishing best practices for sustained collaboration. This meant writing and iterating on a structured set of instructions and documentation that gave the AI clear constraints: what it could decide independently, what required approval, and what was out of scope entirely. Each task was scoped explicitly to prevent the AI from speculating on future requirements or expanding beyond what was asked.

Part of the structure involved managing conversation context deliberately. Long sessions degrade AI output quality as the context window fills — so tasks were kept short and well-scoped, with clear definitions of done, ensuring each conversation stayed focused and never reached the bloated stage where earlier instructions lose influence.

The result is as much a set of reusable collaboration patterns as it is a portfolio site.
