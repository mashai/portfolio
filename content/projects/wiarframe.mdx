Wiarframe is an AR prototyping tool that empowers anyone to design, prototype, and share interactive AR experiences without specialist knowledge. I led development of the client application within a split app/web architecture, owning delivery and publishing of the app platform while working closely with the founder.

My work included building real-time synchronisation with the web editor, optimising asset loading, and delivering a seamless cross-platform experience alongside ongoing R&D and feature development. The project was selected to compete at TechCrunch Disrupt Berlin 2018 (Startup Battlefield) among the year's top startups, and later at the AWE Startup Pitch Competition in San Francisco.
