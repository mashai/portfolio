Gingersnap is a personalised storytelling app for children featuring embedded Unity3D game adventures within a native iOS shell. My work focused on the deep integration layer between Unity and iOS, building robust communication systems that enabled seamless transitions between native and game experiences.

I built editor tools, gameplay frameworks, and the Unity–iOS communication layer to support rapid content creation and iteration by the wider team. The successful alpha release gave the team the foundation they needed to secure continued funding for product development.
