Virtual Realms brought me in as Lead Software Engineer to take their next MMORPG from prototype to investor alpha. The studio had an established mobile MMO, but Legend of Elumia targeted PC and mobile with NFT-based item ownership — characters, equipment and items living on the blockchain rather than the studio's servers. That meant rebuilding the server, auth and database architecture from scratch, alongside new skill and combat systems. I led the programming team, worked closely with artists and animators, and built internal tooling to reduce friction in the art pipeline and keep the team moving.

One of the reasons I was brought in was the blockchain integration that had stalled. I set up a sandbox environment to keep production moving while I worked through the problem, and ended up establishing communication between our servers and the Solana blockchain, putting us back on track.

While overseeing Elumia's development, the studio's other live MMO was hit by a database breach and required immediate attention. We locked down access, restored from clean versions, worked through affected users, and kept monitoring as attacks continued.

The investor deadline was met, allowing production to continue. The project was later acquired by Triumph Games and released on Android.
