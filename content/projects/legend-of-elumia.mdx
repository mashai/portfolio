As Lead Software Engineer at Virtual Realms, I owned both client and server systems for Legend of Elumia, a new MMORPG built on an existing codebase. The scope spanned rapid prototyping, internal tooling, and backend service integrations — including R&D for blockchain-based character systems when third-party solutions failed to meet requirements — through to alpha delivery.

During the engagement I also led the recovery and hardening of the studio's live production infrastructure following a database breach on their existing MMO. This involved securing the environment under active threat, restoring service integrity, and implementing controls to prevent recurrence.
