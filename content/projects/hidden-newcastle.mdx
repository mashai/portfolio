Working at Fluid Pixel, I contributed to Hidden Newcastle — a free iOS and Android app for Tyne & Wear Archives & Museums that guides users through 800 years of the city's often macabre and forgotten history. My work focused on UI and maps features across both platforms.

The app was featured by the BBC: *"Victorian child 'villains' bring Newcastle app to life."*
