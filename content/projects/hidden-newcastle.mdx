Hidden Newcastle is a free mobile app developed for Tyne & Wear Archives & Museums that guides users through 800 years of the city's often macabre and forgotten history. Developed at Fluid Pixel Ltd, I contributed to features, stability, and delivery of the app across iOS and Android.
