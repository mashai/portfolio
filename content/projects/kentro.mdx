Joined OLO Technologies to stabilise and unblock Kentro, a production AR configurator under deadline pressure for enterprise OEM customers. The system had accumulated significant architectural debt that was limiting scalability and threatening delivery commitments.

I re-architected the data model and migrated the platform to Firebase-backed cloud infrastructure, enabling reliable multi-project, multi-device access. I owned both client and backend systems, covering API design, hosting, authentication, and account management through to a successful release.
