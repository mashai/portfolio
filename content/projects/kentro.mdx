Joined OLO Technologies to stabilise and unblock Kentro, a production AR configurator under deadline pressure for enterprise OEM customers. The system had accumulated significant architectural debt that was limiting scalability and threatening delivery commitments.

I re-architected the data model and migrated the platform to Firebase-backed cloud infrastructure, enabling reliable multi-project, multi-device access. I owned the full scope of client and backend systems — including API design, hosting, authentication, and account management — and delivered the platform to a successful release.
