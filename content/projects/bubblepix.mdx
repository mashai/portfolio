I joined Fluid Pixel straight out of university, working as a developer on BubblePix — the companion app for the BubbleScope, an optical accessory that captures 360° images and video through a fisheye lens in a single shot.

The core technical challenge was converting images taken through the fisheye lens into accurate 360° panoramas — working alongside a fellow programmer, I contributed to that processing work and shipped features and fixes across iOS and Android.
