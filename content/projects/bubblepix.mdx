BubblePix is the companion app for the BubbleScope, an optical imaging accessory that attaches to a smartphone to capture 360° bubble images and videos in a single shot. Developed at Fluid Pixel Ltd, I contributed to features, stability, and delivery of the app across Android and iOS platforms.
