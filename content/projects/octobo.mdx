Octobo is a smart plush octopus with an iPad as its face — reacting to touch, scanning NFC story tokens, and connecting to the plush via Bluetooth. I was part of a two-developer team contracted by Thinker-Tinker to build the iOS application.

My contributions included debugging the Bluetooth and NFC libraries, story integration, UI, and the eye animation system that activates when the tablet is placed in the plush.
