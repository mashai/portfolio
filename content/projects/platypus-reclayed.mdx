The challenge was to faithfully recreate a 22-year-old arcade title on a modern engine without access to original source code or documentation. Working from binary analysis and runtime observation, I reconstructed the game's core foundations — including its timing systems, data formats, and gameplay behaviours — with enough accuracy to support testing, modding, and future development.

The reconstruction required deep reverse-engineering of proprietary formats and engine-level systems, alongside careful validation against original hardware behaviour. The result is a maintainable, modern codebase that preserves the original experience while enabling the kind of iterative development the original platform never supported.
